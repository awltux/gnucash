    Saved views

      Report designer

      Build a report from the same query, columns and options the native reports use. Preview it, then save it under a name: saved reports live on the server (outside the book) and appear in the Reports list above.

      Comma or space separated lowercase slugs: letters, digits and hyphens, at most 24 characters and 12 tags. Tags are saved with the report on the server, so every browser on this book sees them; click a tag in the Reports list to filter by it.

      A flat list prints one row per account; the account tree rolls parents up and adds a subtotal; by period prints one row per year or month in the date range.

      Period activity is the amount earned or spent in each period. Balance at period end accumulates from the start of the book, which is what a net-worth or balance-sheet trend needs.

      Each checked account means that account and its whole subtree. Nothing checked means every account in the book.

      Restrict the report to these account types. This survives a different book, unlike an account GUID. Nothing ticked means every type; when both accounts and types are chosen, an account must match both.

      One or two amount columns the chart plots over the table's rows, or all three on a period report: debit, credit and balance are that period's two sides and its net (income, expense and net profit; assets, liabilities and net worth). Balance cannot be combined with just one side. Nothing ticked means the columns shown decide: debit and credit when both are shown, otherwise the balance or the one amount column.
